Privacy

Short version: no ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

What the site stores

The map may save display settings and a map-data version in your browser. The local editor saves your edits in your browser too. This information is not sent to us.

Hosting

Cloudflare hosts and protects the site. Like any web host, it processes normal technical request data such as IP address, browser details, and request time to deliver and secure the service.

Third-party links

If you choose to open a Discord or YouTube link, that service handles its own data under its own privacy policy.

# About the Interactive Loot Map

**Days Gone information, without digging through ten guides.**

A free fan-made map for loot, crafting materials, weapons, throwables, plants, and useful finds across Oregon.

## Built by Bacon FM

I speedrun, test, route, and document Days Gone. This map turns that research into something useful for everyone. [Speedrun.com profile](https://www.speedrun.com/users/BaconFM)

## The point

Find what you need. Know whether it respawns. Spend less time opening random trunks.

More regions, better data, and new guides are on the way when time allows.

**No paywalls. No fake deadlines. Just a fan project getting better.**
